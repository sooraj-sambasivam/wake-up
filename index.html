What it is

Wake Up is an alarm for iPhone and Apple Watch. You set your next alarm on iPhone, then open Wake Up on your Apple Watch and tap Arm on this Watch. At alarm time your Watch taps your wrist, and the taps get more frequent until you dismiss the alarm.

  • Choose Gentle taps that build over three minutes, Steady taps that build over one minute, or Persistent taps every second.
  • Choose no snooze, one five-minute pause, or unlimited pauses.
  • Optional phone sound backup, with a delay you choose.
  • Saved weekday and weekend times, and battery checks before bed.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics. The app sends no data to the developer.

Apple Watch is required for wrist alarms. Saving an alarm on iPhone does not arm it; you arm each night's alarm on your Watch. Wake Up cannot guarantee that you will wake up. Please read the safety notice in the Terms of Use.
